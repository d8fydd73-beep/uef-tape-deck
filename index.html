How to load a game
  1. Plug the USB-C to 3.5mm adapter into the phone and the cassette lead into the BBC’s cassette socket. The motor lead isn’t used.
  2. Turn on Do Not Disturb. A notification sound in the middle of a block will cause a load error.
  3. On the BBC type *TAPE then CHAIN"" (or *RUN for machine code).
  4. Press Play. At each break the deck does what the catalogue says: carry on, pause until you press Play, or wait a set time.
  5. If the BBC shows Data?, Block? or Header?, press Block to rewind one block. The BBC will pick it up again.

To get games, tap Find games, download a game’s zip, then come back and tap Open UEF or zip. You don’t need to extract the zip. Accepts .uef files, compressed or not. Break settings are remembered for each tape on this phone.
